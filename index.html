About

Research at the intersection of machine learning, language, and structured knowledge.

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Iowa. My work focuses on large-scale NLP, representation learning, and knowledge-guided machine learning. I am particularly interested in building models that generalize effectively when supervision is sparse, labels are highly imbalanced, and the output space is large.

My research combines pretrained Transformer models, contrastive learning, graph-based knowledge, and rigorous experimental evaluation. I have worked with datasets containing more than 1.5 million documents and 29,000+ labels.
